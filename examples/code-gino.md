# My video style: Code Gino

- Format: vertical 9:16 (1080x1920), source frame rate.
- Language: Taglish. Preserve my original words and code-switching; never translate or replace my voice.
- Captions: natural phrase chunks, usually 3 to 6 words shown together. Shorter groups are fine at clause boundaries.
- Caption position: upper third, adjusted to keep my face clear. Prefer one line when it fits; split phrases rather than shrinking text until it is hard to read.
- Caption appearance: Inter Bold (700), bright white text on a dark-green backing, with a restrained green accent. Fit the size to the footage; 50px at 1080px wide is a starting point.
- HDR captions: map graphic white independently of the footage. Start with 300-nit white for HLG and check a rendered sample in the intended player; do not let white text become gray.
- Hook: no extra headline or separate intro by default.
- Pace: natural. Keep short pauses and breaths; shorten long dead air without making joins abrupt.
- Mistakes: remove obvious abandoned takes and mistakes only when the surrounding speech stays intact. Keep deliberate repetition.
- Graphics: minimal. No automatic zoom-ins, swipes, motion cards, or effects demonstrations. Add them when explicitly requested for a video.
- Speaker on screen: always visible; preserve my original framing. Place any requested graphics around me without covering my face.
- Color: natural. Preserve the original footage's appearance and HDR color handling; no exposure change or color grade unless I ask.
- Sound: my original voice only. No background music or added sound effects.
- Branding: Code Gino, https://carlogino.com/. Use the website palette: primary green #409147, dark green #05420A, pale green #E2F9E3, white #FFFFFF. Typography: Inter. These values were checked on 2026-10-18; verify the site before adopting later brand changes.
- Branding placement: understated text over the footage, with no separate intro or outro card by default.
- Deliverables: MP4 draft, SRT subtitles, and an editable project. Render a final version after approval.

## My rules

- Brand colors apply only to captions and graphics, never to my skin or the footage.
- Keep the footage color unchanged when improving caption brightness or contrast.
- Preserve the HDR source as the master. Check footage color and caption legibility separately before delivering an edit.
